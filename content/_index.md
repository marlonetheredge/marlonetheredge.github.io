Hello, I'm Marlon Etheredge. I am a PhD researcher in the [Distributed and Parallel Systems (DPS) Group](https://dps.uibk.ac.at/) at the University of Innsbruck. My research focuses on programming paradigms, runtime systems, and optimization techniques for the Cloud-Edge-IoT continuum. Read more about my research in my [publications](/publications/) and [blog](/blog/).

Prior to my PhD, I spent 14 years in the software industry across domains such as high-performance computing, computer vision, game engine development, audio engineering, and TV broadcasting. Read more about my experience in my [CV](/cv/).

For freelance work and hiring enquiries, visit [frnd.at](https://frnd.at).
